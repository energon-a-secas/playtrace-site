A NOTE ON THE INDEX · OCTOBER 2026

A shared history.
An open conversation.

Small projects deserve a place where their progress, players, and ideas can stay together.

Follow the game through its posts

Playtrace brings together games shared on r/aigamedev. A project’s original showcase and later updates sit on one timeline, with a direct link to every source.

We’re collecting the available history from the community’s creation in December 2022. The collection shows its progress as older records arrive. Newest updates appear first; choose Most updates to find projects with a longer development story. The first post starts the timeline, and each later post counts as an update.

Useful feedback, open to everyone

Read the conversation or contribute without signing in. Start with quick multi-select questions, then add an optional note. Tell a maker what you enjoyed, where you got stuck, or what you’d like to try. Your choices, display name, note, and optional ideal price are public.

“Your ideal price” is a suggestion for the maker, with no purchase commitment. Their pricing is always their choice. A Steam store price, when available, is shown separately in USD.

A random edit key stays in your browser so you can update or remove your contribution. Clearing browser data removes that editing access. Older account-based price responses stay private.

A quick rating, or a closer review

Rate a game from one to five stars without signing in. The public average counts one editable rating per browser and game. Use the separate star shortcut to save a private favorite after signing in.

Share a quick review, one question at a time, or follow a project’s custom questionnaire. These answers go privately to the editorial team and may be analyzed with Jev to spot themes. You can edit or remove your answers from the same browser. Public project comments remain a separate conversation.

A game of recognition

Name five games from their screenshots, chosen randomly from 20 curated projects with available builds. Some are demos or works in progress. The quiz uses real game names, checked against their project pages; Reddit post headlines are not quiz answers.

Finishing saves your choices and contributes to recognition counts. Replays count as new rounds, and skipped or unavailable images do not count as wrong guesses. These are guesses, not ratings or verified-player counts. You can remove this browser’s quiz answers from the game’s privacy controls. Quiz identifiers are kept separate from public feedback.

Sources, with their limits visible

The Arctic Shift archive helps preserve the community’s history. Collection starts when the community was created in December 2022. Posts tagged Demo | Project | Workflow are eligible; older posts without a flair need an individual review confirming they showcase a game. News, discussion, promotion, tools, and other explicitly labeled categories are excluded. Records can be delayed or incomplete. Projects are grouped when source authors and project evidence support the match. Closely matching reposts by one maker within 48 hours keep the newest entry, preserving the earlier sources and shared links. Source-described entries await editorial review.

Development stages reflect the maker’s posts and available store evidence. Link checks distinguish missing pages from blocked or unverified destinations. A page loading successfully does not prove its game is playable. Historical showcases can remain useful even when a build is no longer available. A Steam badge means the linked app was found in the store; a released badge requires Steam to list it as released. It does not verify the maker’s identity. Prices are for the US store, can change, and may differ in your region.

From comments to useful themes

Editors can review recent Reddit comments as cited, AI-assisted drafts. Published summaries link each theme to the original comments and identify their time window and sampling limits. They remain separate from visitor feedback and require an editor to approve them.

Keep the picture accurate

Previews belong to their creators. Original videos and GIFs stay linked to their source, with playback where the host supports it. Admins can select a clearer image or store an authorized replacement when a clip opens on a black frame.

Use Request a change at the top of a project to flag its image, link, details, or a removal. Requests go privately to the admin inbox. For wider site issues, send a correction.

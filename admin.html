    How to read this dashboard

    Art, engine and model labels are explicit keyword mentions in titles and retained source text. They may describe an influence, a comparison or a tool the maker did not use. The matcher does not inspect screenshots or infer missing versions. Multiple labels can apply to a project. Always inspect the linked source.

    Charts use original source-post dates with the latest available text, including later edits. Store data and editorial genres describe the present. Deleted source text is excluded from mention extraction; its date may remain in the timeline. Imported history, duplicates, missing flair and unequal text coverage limit comparisons. No measured trend here establishes causation.

    The public dashboard shares catalogue evidence and release dates. Visitor activity, questionnaire responses, account information and editorial notes remain in the admin workspace.
